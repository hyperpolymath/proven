== What this is, and how it works

*What it is.* A short, dated, signed snapshot of what hyperpolymath can honestly
and verifiably claim about *proven* at one exact commit. Nothing here is
marketing and nothing is a promise about the future — those live in the README.
This file is the receipt.

*What the project is.* A library of safe operations written in Idris2 with
dependent types, intended to be fully proven and exposed to other languages
through a Zig FFI. *It is not fully proven today.* The sections below say how far
it is from that.

*How it stays trustworthy.* Every claim below comes from the project's own CI at
the anchor commit, or from a grep run against the tree at that commit, and the
evidence column names which. The file is landed by a signed commit.

*We are fallible.* This is our best honest belief, not a proof of its own
correctness.

== The epistemic contract (read this before you trust _or_ attack)

This document records hyperpolymath's *best belief* at the timestamp below. It is
*not a guarantee of correctness.* The only guarantee is *no intentional
overclaim*. Where the README says more than this file does, this file wins.

*Standing invitation to refute.* Bring a counter-example, a failing run, or a
contradicting source, and open an issue.

== What we do NOT claim

* *"Mathematically proven" (the README) is not affirmed.* `PROOF-NEEDS.adoc`
  (sampled proven-rate) found a discharged theorem in 1 of 32 sampled modules,
  about 3%.
* *Declarations without a body act as postulates.* The recon at 8542e84c counted
  383, 55 of them reached at runtime. #228 and #229 removed some. They were *not
  re-counted at this anchor*.
* *Escape hatches remain in code:* 20 `assert_total` and 2 `assert_smaller`
  (grep, comments excluded, at the anchor). 17 of them are undocumented (SafeXML, SafeYAML and
  SafeRegex parsers), per the #230 triage.
* *The cryptographic hash primitives are stubs.* `src/Proven/SafeCrypto/Hash.idr`
  has its 9 `%foreign` lines commented out, so sha256, sha512, sha3 and blake2b
  have no implementation.
* *The test suite does not build.* `idris2-ci.yml` job `build (0.8.0)` failed at
  a32c3b65, because `tests.ipkg` still calls names renamed in bdb18639
  (2026-05-11). No property test has run since then.
* *Not every module is built.* At 8542e84c the recon found about 79 `.idr` files
  under `src/` in no `.ipkg`, so CI never typechecks them. Not re-counted at this anchor.
* *At 8542e84c (recon, not re-counted here):* about 15 theorem statements are
  weakened by unbound implicits (52 warnings), and 71 "theorems" conclude only `()`.
* *Red checks at the anchor:*
** `governance / Workflow security linter`;
** `governance / Validate Hypatia Baseline`;
** `governance / Trusted-base reduction policy`;
** `GitHub Pages / build`;
** `mirror / mirror-gitea`, `mirror-disroot` and `mirror-bitbucket`.
